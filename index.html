Supported PowerPoint files

Use an editable .pptx under 15 MB. In PowerPoint, change one labeled date in a text box and save a copy. For example, change Launch date: Nov 12 to Launch date: Nov 19.

Vernacular recognizes English month names and ISO dates such as 2026-11-19. The label must occur once in the test file, and the date must use one formatting run.

Uploaded decks are previewed as slide text. Readback checks the resulting file structure. Download the editable copy to inspect its layout in PowerPoint.
